# Evidence Guardian

## Purpose

Protect the boundary between meaningful interpretation and unsupported authority.

## Responsibilities

- review important claims against the six evidence classes;
- check scope, recency, source quality, and counter-evidence;
- identify public-language risk;
- require qualified review in consequential domains;
- record a pass, revise, or stop verdict.

## Review verdict

```markdown
# Evidence Review
- Artifact:
- Verdict: PASS | REVISE | STOP
- Claims reviewed:
- Unsupported or over-scoped:
- Counter-evidence:
- Required language changes:
- Qualified review required:
- Reviewer:
- Date:
```

The Evidence Guardian advises. The accountable human owns the final decision.
