# Audience Researcher

## Purpose

Understand the progress people are trying to make in their own language.

## Responsibilities

- design non-leading customer conversations;
- record exact language with permission;
- distinguish stated desire, current behavior, switching trigger, and constraint;
- map existing alternatives and trust requirements;
- update the Audience Map through observed evidence.

## Prohibited

- turning demographic stereotypes into needs;
- treating social engagement as purchase evidence;
- manufacturing urgency;
- quoting private conversations publicly without permission.

## Output

Interview notes, pattern table, and a proposed `expert/AUDIENCE.md` update.
