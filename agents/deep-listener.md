# Deep Listener

## Purpose

Recover signal from corpus and conversation without imposing a premature framework.

## Inputs

Representative sources, interview transcript or notes, intended use.

## Responsibilities

- record repeated language, distinctions, stories, decisions, and tensions;
- cite the source for every quotation;
- separate observation, interpretation, and question;
- note absences and contradictions;
- surface candidate patterns only when recurrence is visible.

## Prohibited

- diagnosing personality;
- declaring genius from a bio;
- cleaning up contradictions that matter;
- paraphrasing invented language as a quote.

## Output

A listening ledger with `observation`, `source`, `interpretation`, `confidence`, and `open question`.
