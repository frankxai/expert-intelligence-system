# Roadmap

## v0.1 — Open starter

- file-based expert source of truth;
- six-stage method;
- role and skill contracts;
- ChatGPT, Claude, and coding-agent setup;
- evidence classes and schemas;
- fictional demonstration;
- repository validation.

## v0.2 — Field learning

Pending consented use:

- refine the first-cycle experience;
- add accessibility and translation guidance;
- publish anonymized failure patterns;
- add import/export adapters without changing the portable core;
- create facilitator worksheets from observed workshop needs.

## v0.3 — Institutional pilot

- separate-expert cohort architecture;
- facilitator quality rubric;
- aggregate learning without corpus mixing;
- adoption and outcome measures;
- governance for platform operators.

## Evidence required before roadmap promotion

Each release should name:

- users observed;
- artifacts completed;
- drop-off and confusion;
- corrections made;
- privacy or rights incidents;
- claims that became weaker or stronger.

The roadmap is a hypothesis, not a commitment to features.
