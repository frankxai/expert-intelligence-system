# Expert Intelligence System

> Turn your life's work into an intelligence system you own.

Most experts do not have a content problem. They have an intelligence architecture problem.

Their best thinking is spread across courses, calls, notebooks, research, posts, workshops, and years of decisions. A general-purpose AI can search some of it. An Expert Intelligence System makes the underlying method explicit: what the expert sees, how they decide, which claims have support, how they sound, who they serve, and where human judgment must remain.

This repository is a free, open, file-based starter for building that system.

## What you will build

By the end of the first cycle, you will have:

- a **Genius Map** grounded in your real corpus;
- a **Method Map** that turns tacit judgment into a teachable process;
- an **Evidence Map** that separates support, experience, hypothesis, and metaphor;
- an **Audience Map** built around real progress and real constraints;
- a **Voice Protocol** that preserves how you think and speak;
- an **Expert Library** that works across tools;
- a small **agent team** with clear responsibilities and boundaries;
- one useful output: a research brief, offer, course module, newsletter, book chapter, or customer-learning plan.

Your AI does not become you. It works from explicit records you control.

## Start in 15 minutes

1. Download or fork this repository.
2. Open [START-HERE.md](START-HERE.md).
3. Put 3–5 representative source files in a private folder outside the public repository.
4. Give your AI workspace [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) plus the relevant files from `expert/`.
5. Run the first workflow: `skills/expert-intake/SKILL.md`.

No terminal is required. The same Markdown files work in ChatGPT Projects, Claude Projects/Cowork, coding agents, and local folders. See [starter-packs](starter-packs/README.md).

## The method

```text
LISTEN  →  RECLAIM  →  DISTILL  →  ENCODE  →  ACTIVATE  →  COMPOUND
corpus     knowledge    method       system      useful work   learning
```

The six stages are defined in [EXPERT-INTELLIGENCE-METHOD.md](EXPERT-INTELLIGENCE-METHOD.md). The complete operating sequence is in [workflows/README.md](workflows/README.md).

## Choose your path

### I already have a body of work

Use the **Established Expert** path if you have courses, frameworks, clients, research, a substantial audience, or years of recorded work. Start with corpus recovery, method extraction, and evidence mapping.

### I am building my first expert-led business

Use the **Emerging Founder** path if your expertise is real but your business is early. Start with your experience, repeated problem-solving patterns, customer conversations, and one narrow transformation you can responsibly deliver.

Both paths create the same underlying system. The order and depth differ.

## Principles

1. **Corpus before claims.** Do not infer a person's genius from a bio.
2. **Method before automation.** Make judgment explicit before delegating it.
3. **Evidence before authority.** Label the status and source of every important claim.
4. **Voice before volume.** Preserve useful distinctions; do not manufacture generic content.
5. **Files before lock-in.** The system remains readable and portable without a platform.
6. **One useful outcome before scale.** Prove the system on real work.
7. **Human decision rights remain human.** The expert approves publication, claims, offers, and consequential advice.

## What this is not

- not an “AI clone” or digital impersonation;
- not a promise of guaranteed income, virality, or effortless expertise;
- not permission to ingest confidential, licensed, employer-owned, or client-owned material;
- not a substitute for medical, legal, financial, scientific, or other qualified review;
- not another content calendar detached from the expert's method and evidence.
