# Product Definition

## Category

Expert Intelligence Architecture

## Position

For established experts and experienced founders whose valuable thinking is scattered across years of work, Expert Intelligence System is an open method and portable starter that turns their corpus, judgment, voice, audience insight, and evidence into a system they own.

Unlike a generic second brain, content generator, or “AI clone,” it makes the expert's method and decision boundaries explicit, preserves provenance, and works across AI tools.

## Primary user

An established expert with:

- a meaningful body of work;
- repeated client, student, or audience outcomes;
- one or more courses, frameworks, books, communities, or services;
- growing awareness of AI but an incomplete operating model;
- concern about quality, voice, evidence, or platform dependence.

## Secondary user

An experienced practitioner building a first expert-led business. They have real domain exposure and repeated problem solving, but not yet a mature corpus or clear offer.

## Institutional user

An expert platform, education business, accelerator, or community that wants to help several experts become AI-native without forcing them into one generic workflow.

## Job to be done

> Help me make my best knowledge, judgment, and method usable by people and AI, so I can do more meaningful work without reducing my expertise to generic content or losing control of it.

## First proof

A user can complete an intake, map a representative corpus, produce one evidence-aware Expert Map, and use it to improve one real artifact.

## Non-goals for v0.1

- hosted SaaS;
- autonomous publishing;
- model fine-tuning;
- automated scraping of a person's public presence;
- revenue guarantees;
- replacing qualified professional review.
