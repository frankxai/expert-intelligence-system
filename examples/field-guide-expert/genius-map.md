# Genius Map — Mira Vale

**Fictional example. Not a client result.**

## Signal

Mira repeatedly treats local stories as field evidence that must be translated, not merely collected. Her distinctive move is to connect community listening with instructional design while keeping the original context visible.

## Repeated framework

**Story → Decision → Practice**

1. A participant story describes a concrete situation.
2. The group identifies the decision inside the story.
3. The facilitator converts the decision into a practice someone can test.

**Observed in:** fictional Workshop A sections 2 and 5, Workshop B section 3, and Facilitator Guide v2 page 7.

## Cross-domain edge

Community listening × instructional design = context-preserving field guides.

## Productive tension

Mira wants practices to be repeatable, but resists making them universal. The system must carry context and boundary conditions with every practice.

## Freedom path

- **KEEP:** listening, interpreting context, choosing which distinctions matter.
- **DELEGATE:** transcript preparation, formatting, workshop logistics.
- **AUTOMATE:** candidate story indexing, first-pass source tables, template assembly.
- **STOP:** turning every workshop into generic social content.
