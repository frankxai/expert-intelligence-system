# First Asset — 45-Minute Workshop Outline

**Fictional demonstration.**

## Intended progress

Participants turn one consented local story into a practice with a visible boundary.

## Flow

1. **Listen — 8 minutes:** read the story and record only observations.
2. **Locate — 8 minutes:** name conditions, people, and constraints.
3. **Translate — 10 minutes:** write one action another person could test.
4. **Challenge — 8 minutes:** name a context where the action could fail.
5. **Teach — 8 minutes:** pair the action with its context card.
6. **Review — 3 minutes:** confirm consent and decide whether the artifact remains private.
