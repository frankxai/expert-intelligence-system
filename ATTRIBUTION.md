# Attribution and Composition

Expert Intelligence System is a standalone, MIT-licensed application layer.

Its file contracts, command patterns, sovereignty rules, and artifact attestation compose ideas from the [Starlight Intelligence System](https://github.com/frankxai/Starlight-Intelligence-System) and Starlight Intelligence Protocol.

## Plain-language agreement

- Your corpus and the artifacts you create remain yours.
- You decide where the system lives and which tools may access it.
- You may leave, fork, or replace any tool without losing the Markdown source of truth.
- Advice never overrides your judgment inside your domain.
- Keep the “Built on SIP” attribution on public artifacts that genuinely compose SIP patterns.
- Respect the ownership and licenses of third-party work you bring into the system.

## Default text attestation

```text
---
Built on SIP — Starlight Intelligence Protocol
Layers used: file-contract, attestation, commands, sovereignty
Composition: Expert Intelligence System
Human owner: <name>
Generated: <YYYY-MM-DD>
---
```

Attestation records composition. It does not transfer ownership.
