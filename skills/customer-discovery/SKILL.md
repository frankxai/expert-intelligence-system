# Customer Discovery

## Load

- `AGENTS.md`
- `expert/AUDIENCE.md`
- `agents/audience-researcher.md`

## Minimum cycle

Five relevant conversations or five strong existing research artifacts. Public comments and engagement are supporting signals, not a substitute for direct learning.

## Interview prompts

1. What was happening when you first realized this needed to change?
2. What did you try before?
3. What worked, and where did it break?
4. What made the problem important now?
5. What would meaningful progress look like in observable terms?
6. What would make you distrust a person or system offering help?

Do not mention the proposed solution until the current behavior and alternatives are understood.

## Process

1. Define the learning question and participant criteria.
2. Obtain permission for notes, recording, and any public quotation.
3. Record exact language separately from interpretation.
4. Map trigger, desired progress, current behavior, alternatives, anxieties, and trust requirements.
5. Compare across conversations.
6. Propose an Audience Map update.

## Output

- `work/maps/customer-research-plan-<YYYYMMDD>.md`
- `work/learning/customer-patterns-<YYYYMMDD>.md`

## Quality gate

- Questions are not leading.
- Exact words are labeled and permission is recorded.
- Behavior and switching events carry more weight than compliments.
- Dissenting cases remain visible.
- The next experiment changes one variable.
