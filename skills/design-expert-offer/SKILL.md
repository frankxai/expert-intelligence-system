# Design Expert Offer

## Load

- `AGENTS.md`
- `expert/AUDIENCE.md`
- `expert/METHOD.md`
- `expert/EVIDENCE.md`
- `expert/OFFERS.md`
- `expert/BOUNDARIES.md`
- `agents/evidence-guardian.md`

## Entry gate

Stop if the Audience Map is based only on assumptions or the Method Map has no real cases. Route back to customer discovery or method distillation.

## Process

1. Name the specific person, situation, before state, and after state.
2. Select the smallest slice the method can responsibly deliver.
3. Define artifacts, participation, duration, and completion criteria.
4. State fit and non-fit.
5. Connect claims to the Evidence Map.
6. Define proof required before stronger claims.
7. Place the offer in the ladder: open starter, diagnostic, build sprint, studio, or institutional pilot.
8. Draft language without pricing or guarantees unless the business owner explicitly supplies and approves them.

## Output

Write `work/maps/offer-architecture-<slug>-<YYYYMMDD>.md`.

## Quality gate

- The result is observable, not aspirational.
- The offer does not promise what depends on the customer or external conditions.
- The method and evidence are visible.
- Non-fit criteria are clear.
- Pricing, legal terms, and public claims retain human approval.
