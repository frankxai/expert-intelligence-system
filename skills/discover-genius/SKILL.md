# Discover Genius

## Load

- `AGENTS.md`
- `expert/GENIUS.md`
- `expert/VOICE.md`
- `agents/deep-listener.md`
- `agents/methodologist.md`

## Required inputs

At least three representative sources from more than one context. Prefer five. Confirm ownership and intended use.

## Process

1. Create a source table with title, date, context, rights, sensitivity, and relevance.
2. Extract observations with source locations.
3. Mark a framework candidate only when a pattern occurs in at least three source observations.
4. Identify two or more fields the expert repeatedly combines.
5. Collect 5–10 exact voice samples with source references.
6. Sort observed activities into KEEP, DELEGATE, AUTOMATE, and STOP. Do not leave a bucket empty without explaining the evidence gap.
7. Record contradictions and low-confidence interpretations.
8. Draft a Genius Map proposal. Do not overwrite `expert/GENIUS.md`.

## Output

Write:

- `work/maps/genius-map-<slug>-<YYYYMMDD>.md`
- `work/maps/voice-observations-<slug>-<YYYYMMDD>.md`

Each candidate finding must include `observation`, `sources`, `interpretation`, and `confidence`.

## Quality gate

- No invented quotation or source.
- No framework with fewer than three observations.
- At least one meaningful tension is preserved.
- The cross-domain edge names actual fields present in the corpus.
- A human promotion decision is requested.
