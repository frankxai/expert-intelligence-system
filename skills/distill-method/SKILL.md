# Distill Method

## Load

- `AGENTS.md`
- `expert/METHOD.md`
- latest approved Genius Map or proposal;
- `agents/methodologist.md`
- `agents/evidence-guardian.md`

## Required inputs

At least three examples of the expert addressing the target problem. Include one weak, failed, or contradictory case when possible.

## Process

1. Describe the observable before and after states.
2. Sequence the interventions that recur across cases.
3. At each stage, record input, judgment, action, output, and failure mode.
4. Convert tacit judgment into conditional rules:
   - when;
   - choose;
   - because;
   - do not when;
   - ask the human when.
5. Record diagnostic signals and recovery paths.
6. Separate:
   - observed practice;
   - practitioner hypothesis;
   - teaching metaphor;
   - supported external claim.
7. Test the method against the contradictory case.

## Output

Write `work/maps/method-map-<slug>-<YYYYMMDD>.md`.

## Quality gate

- Every stage has an observable input and output.
- Important judgment is not hidden behind “use intuition.”
- Boundary conditions are explicit.
- A negative or contradictory case has been considered.
- Evidence status is visible.
- The method can stop and escalate.
