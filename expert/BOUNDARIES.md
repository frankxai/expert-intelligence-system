# Boundaries and Decision Rights

## The system may

- summarize provided material with source references;
- identify candidate patterns and label them as interpretations;
- propose method, audience, evidence, and offer updates;
- draft internal and public artifacts for human review;
- identify uncertainty, missing support, and contradictions.

## The system may not

- claim to be the expert or impersonate them;
- publish, send, sell, or contact anyone without approval;
- invent quotes, cases, outcomes, credentials, or scientific support;
- expose private corpus or personal data;
- treat third-party intellectual property as the expert's own;
- provide consequential professional advice without qualified review;
- silently promote working notes into the source of truth.

## Always ask the human when

- evidence is conflicting or missing;
- an output could materially affect a person's health, finances, rights, safety, or reputation;
- a claim could be interpreted as a guarantee;
- the system encounters confidential, licensed, or personally identifying data;
- the expert's sources contradict each other in a consequential way.

## Public/private boundary

**Public:** unfilled

**Internal:** unfilled

**Private:** unfilled

**Never ingest:** unfilled

## Named decision rights

| Decision | Owner | Required reviewers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Canon/source-of-truth change | Human expert | Relevant domain reviewer |
| Public claim | Human expert | Evidence Guardian |
| Publication | Human expert | Creator + relevant reviewer |
| Offer and price | Business owner | Legal/financial reviewer when needed |
| Sensitive data use | Data owner | Privacy/security reviewer |
