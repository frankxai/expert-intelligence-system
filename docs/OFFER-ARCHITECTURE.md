# Offer Architecture

The free repository is the public product. Paid work, if offered, should increase diagnosis, implementation depth, and accountability—not hide the basic method.

## Category statement

**Expert Intelligence Architecture** turns a body of work into a portable system of method, evidence, voice, audience knowledge, and agent workflows.

## Message

> Turn your life's work into an intelligence system you own.

Supporting line:

> Your courses, conversations, research, stories, and methods already contain a system. Find it, structure it, and put it to work without flattening your voice or handing your knowledge to one platform.

## Ladder

### 1. Open Expert Intelligence Starter

**Progress:** understand the architecture and create a first map.

**Artifacts:** repository, starter packs, Expert Map templates, skills, and examples.

**Proof:** the user can inspect and run it without a sales call.

### 2. Expert Map Session

**Progress:** identify the highest-leverage gap in corpus, method, evidence, audience, or activation.

**Artifacts:** intake card, live map, and one 30-day build decision.

**Boundary:** diagnostic, not a promise to build the full system in one conversation.

### 3. Expert Intelligence Sprint

**Progress:** build the core source of truth and prove it on one artifact.

**Artifacts:** corpus/reclamation map, Genius Map, Method Map, Evidence Map, agent/skill configuration, and first useful output.

### 4. Expert Intelligence Studio

**Progress:** apply and improve the system across research, publishing, courses, books, customer learning, and team operations.

**Artifacts:** a governed pipeline and learning cadence.

### 5. Institutional Pilot

**Progress:** equip a bounded cohort of experts with separate, sovereign systems.

**Artifacts:** orientation, facilitator kit, individual maps, one proof task per expert, and an aggregate learning report.

## Conversation opener

> Show me one piece of work you are proud of, one recurring problem people come to you for, and one place your best thinking is currently trapped.

The goal is deep listening and a useful map, not a surprise pitch.

## Proof strategy

Before stronger commercial claims:

1. run five expert map sessions;
2. document the before, artifact, decision improved, and consented feedback;
3. distinguish subjective impact from observable operational change;
4. publish only with explicit permission;
5. record non-results and revise the method.

Pricing and guarantees are intentionally absent from the open architecture. The business owner decides them with legal and market context.
