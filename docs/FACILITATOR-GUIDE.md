# Facilitator Guide

This guide supports a 90-minute workshop or the Expert Intelligence segment of a longer expert-business session.

## Outcome

Each participant leaves with:

- a named intelligence problem;
- a five-source corpus plan;
- one map started;
- one dated artifact;
- a clear privacy and evidence boundary.

## Before the session

Ask participants to bring:

1. one piece of work they are proud of;
2. one transcript, workshop, or customer conversation;
3. one recurring framework or decision process;
4. one example of real impact;
5. one idea they may have changed their mind about.

They should remove confidential and personal information.

## 90-minute flow

### 0–10 — Reframe

Most experts do not need another AI tool. They need a usable architecture for the intelligence already present in their work.

### 10–25 — Corpus and rights

Participants inventory five sources and mark owner, sensitivity, date, and intended use.

### 25–45 — Deep-listening pair

Use three prompts:

- What do people repeatedly come to you for?
- What distinction do you make that changes how they see the problem?
- Tell me about one case that did not fit your usual method.

The listener records exact language separately from interpretation.

### 45–65 — Expert Map

Draft one candidate repeated pattern, one cross-domain edge, one boundary, and one evidence question.

### 65–80 — Activation

Choose one real artifact the map should improve.

### 80–90 — Commitment and trust

Name the artifact, date, human reviewer, and data boundary.

## Four-hour integration

For a longer “build an expert business” session, use this system as the architecture layer:

1. expertise and audience problem;
2. corpus and knowledge recovery;
3. method and evidence;
4. offer and first proof;
5. AI workspace setup;
6. 30-day activation plan.

Do not spend the session installing tools. Demonstrate one file-aware workflow, then return to the expert's decision.

## Facilitator boundaries

- do not publicly expose participant corpus;
- do not call a candidate pattern “their genius” without review;
- do not encourage premature medical, financial, legal, or scientific authority;
- do not sell an implementation offer during someone else's vulnerable reflection;
- disclose affiliations and commercial next steps clearly.
