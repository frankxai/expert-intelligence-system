# Ten-Minute Demonstration

## Goal

Show that the system can recover a useful distinction from real material and turn it into a traceable action.

## Setup

Use the fictional files under `examples/field-guide-expert/` or a participant's material with explicit permission.

## Script

1. **Show the problem:** three source types that do not currently form a system.
2. **Show the contract:** open `AGENTS.md` and point to corpus, evidence, privacy, and human gates.
3. **Run one skill:** `discover-genius` on representative excerpts.
4. **Inspect the map:** distinguish source observation from interpretation.
5. **Challenge it:** ask for the contradictory case and evidence status.
6. **Activate it:** use the map to outline one lesson or research brief.
7. **Show portability:** open the same Markdown files in a second AI workspace.

## What to say

> The impressive part is not that AI can write. The important part is that the method, evidence, boundaries, and corrections now live in files the expert can inspect and carry elsewhere.

## What not to claim

- that the system has captured the whole person;
- that one demonstration proves business results;
- that the fictional example is a client outcome;
- that portability means every model will produce identical work.
